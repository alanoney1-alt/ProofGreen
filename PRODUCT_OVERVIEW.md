## How It Works

### Step 1: Log Your Job (30 seconds)
- Take a photo of materials/equipment

- You verify accuracy and submit

### Step 2: Automatic Tracking
Everything is calculated automatically:
- Waste diversion rate (% recycled vs landfill)
- Carbon offset (lbs CO2 avoided)
- Donation value (tax-deductible amounts)
- Compliance status (EPA, state regulations)

### Step 3: Generate Reports
One click to create:
- Professional ESG reports
- Customer sustainability receipts
- Contract bid documentation
- Public shareable links
